QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Who can attend?

1st-year students, complete beginners, and students from all branches can attend.

Do I need prior C or programming knowledge?

No. This workshop is designed for beginners, and prior programming knowledge is not required.

Is the workshop free?

Yes, entry to the workshop is free.

Do I need to bring a laptop?

A laptop is highly recommended, but it is not mandatory.

What if I don't have a laptop?

Computers will be provided in the lab for participants who do not have a laptop.

Can students from other branches attend?

Yes. Students from all branches are welcome.

Can 2nd, 3rd or 4th year students attend?

No.It's only for First Years.

How do I confirm my seat?

Your seat is confirmed after you complete the required registration process/task send through E-mail.

Are seats limited?

Please check the registration form or event organizers for the latest availability.

Will I receive anything after completing the workshop?

Any post-workshop details will be shared by the organizers.
